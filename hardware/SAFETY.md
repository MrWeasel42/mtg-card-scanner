# Safety

**The power supply runs on wall power (120/230 V AC). Mistakes can cause electric shock or fire.**

- Unplug the machine before opening the electronics bay or touching any wiring.
- Wire the AC side (inlet → power supply L, N and ground) only if you're comfortable with mains wiring. Kits ship
  this harness pre-made. Never probe the L or N terminals while plugged in.
- Use a certified (UL/CE) power supply, inlet and cord, with the fuse in place. The ground wire must be connected.
- Keep the power supply's terminal cover on and the bay closed while the machine is running.
- Moving parts: belts, rollers and bin doors can pinch fingers. Keep hands out while it runs.
- 3D-printed parts near the power supply must be PETG or better (PLA softens with heat).
- This is a hobby machine with no warranty. Don't leave it running unattended for long periods.

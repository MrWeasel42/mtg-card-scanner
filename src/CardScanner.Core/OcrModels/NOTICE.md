# OCR models

`text_det.onnx` and `text_rec.onnx` are PaddleOCR PP-OCRv3 text detection and recognition models
(converted to ONNX by the RapidOCR project). They are used to read card names and collector lines.

- PaddleOCR: https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR (Apache License 2.0)
- RapidOCR: https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR (Apache License 2.0)
